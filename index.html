03 / WHO

About

Some places feel familiar even when you've never been there.

That's the feeling I'm interested in.

I'm Evol Ezod / DOZE, an independent music producer and game composer from Sunderland, UK.

I've been making music for around four years, working across game soundtracks, ambient music, horror, retro, experimental music and other styles depending on what a project needs.

My main focus is music that feels nostalgic, lonely, strange or slightly forgotten. Think old Roblox games, empty corridors, childhood memories you can't quite place and worlds that feel like they've been waiting for somebody to come back.
